## Scenario 1: CFO needs result in 30 minutes on what-if on 8% price increase followed by 2% customer loss

**Tool recommended:Excel**
**Justification:** Excel would allow to build a model within 30 minutes with simple change of data 

## Scenario 2: Find a daily dashboard of each region's churn rate against target 30 people
**Tool recommended: SQL and BI tool**
**Justification:** SQL will allow us to compare data of churn rate against the source data. BI tool's output is a recurring dashboard that allows multiple people, even non-technical, to check on it.

## Scenario 3: Identity which of the 14,000 customer tickets from Q3 mention "shipping delay" and category by sentiment
**Tool recommended: Python**
**Justification:** Python allows to search by Quarters and search specifically by string.

## Scenario 4: A CSV of 1,200 row of data and quick view of which are most popular and back-of-envelope revenue estimate
**Tool recommended: Excel**
**Justification:** Data is less than 100,000 rows of data and one-off question.

## Scenario 5: Build a reusable layer of transformations that other analyst will query which be run nightly and be tested

**Justification:** dbt will allow other analyst to be reuse

## Scenario 6: Know the results with confidence interval of average order value differs between users who arrived from email vs. paid social over the last 90 days 
**Tool recommended: Python**
**Justification:** Python will be able to calculate statistic analysists that find summary statistics from a dataset.

## Scenario 7: Reusable one-page printable summary last quarter's performance for 15 store locations
**Tool recommended: Excel**
**Justification:** 15 stores and fixed layout. Quick and easy with just inputting new numbers when next quarter arrive

## Scenario 8: Recurring Monday funnel analysis on about 600 million rows of click-level data per month

**Justification:** SQL will compete through millions of  data and find data on different tables. The BI tool will read the summary and refresh each Monday for people who need to see the data.
